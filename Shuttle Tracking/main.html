How Our Shuttle Tracking Services came about

Our Shuttle Tracking System is a web application developed by a fellow CPUT student SCELO KEVIN NYANDENI who experienced firsthand the frustration of unreliable shuttle schedules. The inconsistency—sometimes shuttles leaving too early, sometimes late, and rarely on time—made planning commutes difficult for students and staff alike. Motivated by the need for a more dependable and transparent system, this application was created to provide real-time tracking, accurate arrival predictions, and schedule updates

With this platform, users can monitor shuttle movements, reduce uncertainty, and plan their travel with confidence. Our goal is to enhance convenience, minimize wait times, and ensure that no one misses their ride due to unpredictable departures.This system leverages real-time GPS tracking to keep users informed about shuttle locations and estimated arrival times. By accessing the web application, students and staff can check live updates, reducing the stress of uncertainty and last-minute rushes. Whether you're on campus or heading to a pickup point, you’ll always know exactly when your shuttle is arriving.

Designed with user convenience in mind, the platform is intuitive and accessible on both desktop and mobile devices. It aims to improve campus transportation by ensuring better time management and reliability. With this app, we’re taking a step toward a smarter, more efficient commuting experience for everyone at CPUT.
